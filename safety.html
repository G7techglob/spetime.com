YOUR WELL-BEING MATTERS

Safety Guide

Protect your privacy, respect boundaries, and make thoughtful decisions when exploring new connections.

Adults only: Spetime is intended for people aged 18 and older. The website's age notice is not formal age verification.

1. Protect Your Personal Information

  • Keep your home address, passwords, financial details, and identification documents private.
  • Be careful about sharing your phone number, workplace, daily routine, or live location.
  • Use strong, unique passwords for your online accounts.
  • Think carefully before sharing photographs that reveal your home, workplace, or other identifying details.

2. Watch Out for Scams

Be cautious if someone asks for money, gift cards, banking details, verification codes, or cryptocurrency in exchange for companionship, affection, or a promised meeting.

  • Do not send money because someone pressures you or creates an emergency.
  • Never share your password, PIN, or one-time security code.
  • Be suspicious of requests to move quickly to another platform or keep a relationship secret.
  • Remember that photographs and messages alone do not prove someone's identity.

3. Consent and Respect

Every interaction must be voluntary and respectful. Consent must be freely given and can be withdrawn at any time.

  • Respect other people's decisions and personal boundaries.
  • Do not pressure anyone into a conversation, meeting, or activity.
  • Never share another person's intimate images or private information without permission.
  • Do not threaten, harass, stalk, blackmail, or impersonate anyone.

4. Meeting Someone in Person

If you choose to meet someone, consider these precautions:

  • Meet in a busy public place for the first meeting.
  • Arrange your own transportation and keep a way to leave safely.
  • Tell someone you trust where you are going and when you expect to return.
  • Keep your phone charged and accessible.
  • Avoid sharing your home address until you feel it is safe to do so.
  • Leave if you feel uncomfortable, pressured, or unsafe.

5. Images and Videos

Do not assume that a picture or video accurately represents the person, situation, or intentions described. Content can be edited, misleading, or used without permission.

Do not create, upload, request, or distribute sexual content involving minors or intimate content shared without consent. Report suspected exploitation or unlawful content to the appropriate authorities and platform where it appears.

6. If Something Feels Wrong

  • Stop communicating with the person if you feel unsafe.
  • Keep relevant messages or evidence if you need to report a threat or scam, provided it is safe and lawful to do so.
  • Block the person where the service allows it.
  • Contact your bank promptly if you have shared financial information or made a suspicious payment.
  • Report threats, extortion, abuse, or suspected crimes to the appropriate local authorities.

7. Your Safety on Spetime

Spetime provides website information and selected media. Unless a feature is specifically provided, do not assume that people, images, identities, or claims have been independently verified, or that Spetime can supervise or guarantee an interaction.

The website's contact form is not connected to a message delivery service at present. Submitting that form will not deliver a safety report or emergency request.

8. Emergency Help

If you are in immediate danger, move to a safer location if possible and contact local emergency services or someone you trust. Do not rely on this website for emergency assistance.

9. Related Information

10. Contact
