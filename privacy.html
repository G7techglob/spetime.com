Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spetime ("we", "us", or "our") approaches personal information when visitors access our website. Please read it before using the site.

Important: This policy describes our intended privacy practices. It must be reviewed and updated to reflect the website's actual hosting, analytics, contact services, and other technologies before the site is launched fully.

1. Information You Provide

Our website may invite visitors to provide information such as a name, email address, subject, or message when contacting us. The contact form is not currently connected to a message-delivery service. Submitting it does not presently send a message to Spetime.

Do not submit passwords, financial details, identity documents, intimate images, or other sensitive information through an unconnected form.

2. Automatically Collected Information

Our hosting provider or other services used by the site may process technical information such as browser type, device information, IP address, requested pages, and security logs. The information actually collected depends on the services configured for the website.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website may use browser storage or cookies to support features. For example, the current age-confirmation interface may store a temporary confirmation in your browser session. This is not identity or age verification.

We do not claim that analytics, advertising cookies, or other tracking technologies are active unless they have actually been configured.

4. How Information May Be Used

Where applicable, information may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the website.
  • Respond to enquiries after a working contact service is configured.
  • Investigate reported content or security concerns.
  • Prevent misuse, fraud, or unlawful activity.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not promise that information is never shared. Information may be processed by hosting providers, technical service providers, or competent authorities where required by law. Any additional sharing practices must be disclosed if introduced.

6. Data Security

We intend to use reasonable safeguards to protect information under our control. However, no website or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Information should be kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for its stated purpose or to meet legal requirements. Actual retention periods will depend on the services and records maintained by Spetime.

8. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, or information about the handling of your personal data. You may also have the right to object to certain processing or make a complaint to a relevant authority.

These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions. We will need a working contact channel to receive and respond to privacy requests.

9. Children's Privacy and Adult Content

Spetime is intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not intend the website for children. The age notice on the site is not a reliable age-verification system. Do not upload or submit content involving minors or any person who has not consented.

10. Third-Party Websites

Our website may link to external websites. We do not control their privacy practices. Review the privacy policies of those services before providing them with personal information.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the website develops. Changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

12. Contact Us About Privacy

For privacy questions or requests, visit our Contact Us page. Because the contact form is not connected yet, it cannot currently deliver your request. A working contact channel must be provided before launch.
